Legacy & Vision

What I hope my teaching leaves with students and the school community.

I think about my professional legacy in terms of the students I have taught, the responsibilities I have taken on, the knowledge I have shared, and the relationships I have built through teaching.

Teaching With Purpose

Teaching is more than what happens during one lesson.

I see teaching as a responsibility that continues beyond preparing and delivering individual lessons. My work includes teaching English, communicating with students, advising and mentoring learners, working with colleagues, and taking part in wider school responsibilities.

Personal Motto
“Teach with purpose, inspire with passion.”

This motto expresses how I want to approach my work: with a clear purpose in teaching and with genuine care for the students I work with.

Core Values

Three values I try to bring to my work.

Commitment, optimism, and collaboration are values I try to maintain in my teaching, my responsibilities at school, and my work with students and colleagues.

01 / COMMITMENT
C

Committed

Staying dedicated to teaching, supporting students, and carrying out the responsibilities I take on at school.

02 / OPTIMISM
O

Optimistic

Remaining positive when challenges arise and continuing to look for ways to improve my teaching and support students.

03 / COLLABORATION
C

Collaborative

Working with students and colleagues and recognizing that many school activities are stronger when people contribute together.

Professional Contribution

My responsibilities have extended beyond classroom teaching.

Alongside teaching English, I have taken on responsibilities in school leadership, student advising, coordination, and educational and community-oriented activities. These roles have allowed me to contribute to school life in different ways.

01 Teaching English and advising students.
02 Serving as a department head and unit leader.
03 Working as a homeroom teacher and school management member.
04 Taking part in educational and community-oriented activities.
Continuing Learning

I continue to learn alongside my teaching.

My professional learning has included English language teaching, pedagogy, assessment, classroom management, ICT, and educational technology. These areas connect directly with the practical work I do as a teacher.

01 Developing my knowledge of English language teaching.
02 Learning about pedagogy and classroom practice.
03 Developing my knowledge of assessment and classroom management.
04 Using ICT and educational technology in my work.
Future Vision

I want to keep developing as an English language teacher.

My professional vision is to continue developing as an innovative English language teacher, make effective use of technology, and produce high-quality English language learning materials that can support students.

Innovation Continue exploring approaches that can make English teaching more useful and engaging for students.
Technology Continue learning how technology can support teaching and learning.
Learning Materials Prepare high-quality English language learning materials for students.
Influence Continue supporting students and contributing to the educational communities in which I work.
A Lasting Principle

I want my teaching to leave students with something they can use.

Throughout my teaching career, I have tried to give students opportunities to learn, practise English, communicate, and develop greater confidence in using the language. That is the kind of work I want to continue building on in the years ahead.
