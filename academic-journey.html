My journey as an English language teacher has developed through university study, classroom teaching, school responsibilities, and continued learning. The experiences on this page show how my academic preparation and professional work have developed over time.

01 / ACADEMIC PREPARATION

English Language Education

My first degree and later study in TEFL have given me the academic foundation I have needed throughout my teaching career. I continue to draw on that preparation when planning lessons, working with students, and thinking about how English can be taught more effectively.

02 / CONTINUOUS DEVELOPMENT

Professional Learning

My professional learning has covered areas that are directly related to classroom work, including English language teaching, pedagogy, assessment, classroom management, ICT, and educational technology. These areas continue to influence how I prepare lessons and respond to the practical needs of teaching.

Professional Perspective

I am still learning from the work I do.

My academic studies and years of classroom experience have shaped the way I teach English. I continue to think about how students participate in lessons, how they use English to communicate, and how I can make classroom activities more useful to them. For me, professional development is not separate from teaching; it is part of becoming a better teacher through study, practice, and reflection.
