Teaching Practice

How I teach English through interaction and communication.

I teach English with an emphasis on participation, communication, and practical use of the language. I want students to have opportunities to speak, ask questions, work with others, and connect what they learn in class with situations outside the classroom.

“I enjoy teaching English as it gives me much opportunity to have good interaction with my students in class and out of the classroom.”
Philosophy & Approach

I want students to take part in the lesson.

I do not see English teaching as simply explaining a lesson and asking students to remember it. Students need chances to use the language, respond to questions, discuss ideas, work with classmates, and learn from mistakes. My approach is to give students an active role while I guide them and provide support when they need it.

01 / Philosophy

Students should have a voice in the classroom.

I try to make my lessons student centered by giving learners opportunities to speak, respond, ask questions, and work with one another. I also pay attention to students who need more support, because participation is not the same for every learner.

02 / Approach

English should be used, not only studied.

I use the Communicative Approach and try to connect classroom activities with situations in which students may actually need to use English. This helps move lessons beyond memorising language rules and gives students opportunities to practise communication.

Classroom Practice

What I try to make possible in my classroom.

01

Interaction

I value the interaction that happens when students and teachers exchange ideas, ask questions, and respond to one another. I also believe that getting to know students and listening to their needs helps me understand how to support them.

02

Communication

I use the Communicative Approach to give students opportunities to use English for practical purposes. Connecting lessons with real-life situations can help students see English as something they can use, not only something they study for an examination.

03

Learner Development

My work with students also includes advising, mentoring, and encouragement. I want students to develop their English while becoming more willing to participate, ask for help, and take responsibility for their own learning.

18 Years teaching English in Grade 12

I have taught English for the last 18 years in Grade 12.
