Research & Professional Learning

Using research and reflection to think more carefully about teaching.

Research is one part of my academic and professional development as an English language teacher. My interest in research comes from the questions that arise from teaching, learning, classroom interaction, and the practical challenges students and teachers face.

Research Profile

Research helps me examine the work I do as a teacher.

I have completed research work as part of my academic and professional development. My experience in English language teaching gives me a practical starting point for thinking about what happens in the classroom, how students participate, and how teaching approaches can be examined and improved.

Research Perspective

Classroom experience gives me questions worth exploring.

Connecting what I observe with what I learn

Teaching gives me opportunities to notice how students respond to lessons, where they participate confidently, and where they need additional support. I see research as a way of looking more closely at these kinds of questions and connecting classroom experience with academic learning and professional reflection.

Teaching Practice I reflect on classroom experiences and consider how teaching approaches affect student participation.
Learner Development I am interested in how students develop confidence, participate in learning, and build their English.
Professional Learning I use study and reflection to continue developing my knowledge and practice as a teacher.
Educational Practice I try to connect what I learn through professional experience with the practical work of teaching.
Areas of Practice

The areas I keep returning to in my teaching and learning.

01 / LANGUAGE

English Language Learning

I am interested in how students develop their English, particularly their ability to communicate, participate with confidence, and use language in practical situations.

02 / PEDAGOGY

Teaching & Pedagogy

My interest in pedagogy includes student participation, communication, classroom interaction, and the different ways teachers can support students during the learning process.

03 / PRACTICE

Classroom Practice

My classroom experience gives me a practical basis for thinking about teaching, noticing students' needs, and considering how classroom practice can be developed over time.

Professional Development

I continue to learn about the parts of teaching that matter in practice.

My professional development has included areas that connect directly with my work as an English language teacher. I have continued to develop my knowledge of English language teaching, pedagogy, assessment, classroom management, ICT, and educational technology.
