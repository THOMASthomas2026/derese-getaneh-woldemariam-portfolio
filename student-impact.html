Student Impact

Supporting students as they learn and use English.

My work with students focuses on helping them participate, communicate, ask questions, and develop their English through classroom practice. I also try to support students through advising, mentoring, and interaction outside regular lessons.

A Learner-Centered Practice

I want students to take part in their own learning.

My teaching is student centered and communicative. I try to connect English lessons with real-life situations and give students opportunities to participate, communicate, and practise the language themselves.

Grade 12 Experience
18

Years teaching English in Grade 12.

I have taught English in Grade 12 for 18 years. This experience has given me many years of working directly with students at an important stage of their secondary education.

The relationship with students is an important part of teaching.

I have described being recognized by my own students every year. For me, these interactions are part of what makes teaching worthwhile and remind me that teaching involves more than delivering lessons.
