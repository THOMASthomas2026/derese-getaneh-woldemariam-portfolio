About Me

Derese Getaneh Woldemariam

English Language Teacher

My name is Derese Getaneh Woldemariam. I am an English Language Teacher with experience teaching Grade 12 students and working in different school responsibilities. I enjoy teaching English because it gives me opportunities to interact with students, support their learning, and help them become more confident in using the language. My work has also included student advising, school leadership, coordination, educational projects, and continued professional learning.

Professional Focus

Teaching is more than delivering a lesson.

For me, teaching is also about what happens between the lesson plans and the final assessment. It means giving students opportunities to speak, ask questions, make mistakes, receive guidance, and gradually become more confident in using English. I try to make my classroom a place where students participate rather than simply listen, and where English is used as a language for communication, not only as a subject to study.

01 / TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Students should have an active role in learning.

I believe students learn more effectively when they are given opportunities to take part in the lesson. In my English classes, I encourage students to ask questions, share their ideas, work with their classmates, and use the language themselves. My role is not only to explain the lesson, but also to guide students, identify where they need support, and help them become more confident and independent learners.

02 / REAL-LIFE LEARNING

English should be useful beyond the classroom.

Learning English becomes more valuable when students can connect what they study with situations they may encounter in everyday life, further education, and future work. I therefore try to give students opportunities to practise reading, writing, speaking, and vocabulary in ways that encourage them to use English rather than simply remember rules. I also value interaction with students outside formal lesson time, because learning does not stop when the classroom lesson ends.

Personal Approach

I want my students to leave the classroom able to use what they have learned.

Teaching English is not only about completing a syllabus or preparing students for an examination. I want students to have opportunities to practise what they learn, communicate with others, ask questions, and gradually become more confident in using English. That is the approach I continue to develop through my teaching, learning, and work with students.
