Personal Portfolio · English Language Teacher

About my work as an English language teacher.

This page brings together the reasons I teach, the responsibilities I have taken on, and the values that shape the way I work with students and colleagues.

Why I Teach

I value the interaction that teaching creates.

I enjoy teaching English because it gives me many opportunities to interact with my students, both in the classroom and outside it. I value the questions students ask, the discussions that develop during lessons, and the opportunity to understand where they need support.

For me, teaching is also a responsibility to help students use what they learn. I try to make lessons active and practical, while continuing to learn from my students, my colleagues, and my own experience as a teacher.

“Teach with purpose, inspire with passion.”
Personal Values

What guides the way I work.

Committed Optimist Collaborative

I try to be consistent in the responsibilities I take on, remain positive when students or colleagues face challenges, and work with others when a shared effort can produce better results. These values influence how I approach teaching, teamwork, and my wider responsibilities at school.

Responsibilities

My work has extended beyond regular classroom teaching.

Teaching has been one part of my work at school. I have also taken on leadership, coordination, communication, and student-support responsibilities. These roles have given me opportunities to work with colleagues, support school activities, and take responsibility for work that serves the wider school community.
