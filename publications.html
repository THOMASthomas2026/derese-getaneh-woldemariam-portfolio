Writing Perspective

My classroom experience influences what I write.

Teaching gives me a clear sense of the areas where students need explanation, practice, and additional support. Writing allows me to turn some of that experience into learning material that students can return to outside the classroom.

01 / LEARNING

Giving students more to practise with

A learning guide can give students material they can return to after a lesson. I see written resources as a way of giving learners additional opportunities to review language and practise what they have studied.

02 / PRACTICE

Writing from classroom experience

The questions students ask and the difficulties they encounter in learning English can influence the kind of material I prepare. Classroom teaching therefore gives me practical experience to draw on when I write.

03 / CONTRIBUTION

Sharing what I have prepared

Writing allows the work I prepare as a teacher to take a form that can be used beyond a single classroom lesson. A guide book can give students another place to review ideas and continue their study.
